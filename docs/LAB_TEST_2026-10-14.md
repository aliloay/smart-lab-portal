### C. Two people (participants 1 + 2), repeat the whole sequence 3 times
Note who is who at the start. Then:
1. enter one after the other; 2. walk past each other (cross paths); 3. both sit;
4. swap seats; 5. one stands partly in front of the other (occlusion) for ~5 s;
6. one leaves the view, comes back after ~20 s; 7. both leave.
- [ ] After every step write each box's name. PASS = names still on the right people.
      A swap, a duplicate name, or a name on the wrong person = FAIL with the time.
      A box with no name / "Unknown" when the face is not visible = UNCERTAIN, not FAIL.
- [ ] Section C passes if all 3 repetitions have 0 FAIL.
