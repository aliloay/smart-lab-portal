SMART LAB - 4 PRINTED ENCLOSURES (2026-10-25)
All fits = part + 0.3 mm. Heat-set insert holes (4.0) and self-tap pilots (M2.5 2.2, M2 1.6) are
undersize on purpose so they grip. Every box: 0 overlaps in the SolidWorks assembly check.
Printer: Elegoo Neptune 4 (225 x 225 bed) - every part fits.

HOW TO PRINT EACH PART
1_MasterDoor
  DoorEnclosure_Body.STL            back face down, no supports (only short bridges)
  DoorEnclosure_FrontPanel.STL      same way you printed the fit coupon (raised text + holders behind)
  DoorEnclosure_ElectronicsPlate.STL flat, no supports
2_Environment
  EnvEnclosure_Body.STL             back face down, no supports (45-degree corner walls print fine)
  EnvEnclosure_FrontPanel.STL       same way as the door panel / coupon
  EnvEnclosure_ElectronicsPlate.STL flat, no supports
3_ConsumablesBin
  BinEnclosure_Body.STL             upright, open bottom on the bed, supports "build plate only"
                                    (they are all inside and pull out through the open bottom)
  BinEnclosure_Floor.STL            TOP face down (feet up), no supports
  BinEnclosure_CupRing.STL          flat, no supports (optional part)
4_ReusableBinDock
  1_BinDock_Tray.STL                floor down, no supports
  2_BinDock_PedalCover.STL          flat, no supports
  3_BinDock_Body.STL                standing on its flat bottom, open top up, no supports
  4_BinDock_Lid.STL                 flat face down, text up, no supports
  5_BinDock_ElectronicsPlate.STL    flat, no supports

  M16 cable glands (with nut) .................................  6  (door 1, environment 1, consumables 1, dock 3)
  M12 cable glands (with nut) .................................  3  (door: lock, door contact, spare)
  Wall screws ~4 mm x 30 + wall plugs ..........................  8  (door 4 ears, environment 4 ears)
  Zip ties 2.5-3 mm wide, 100-150 mm ...........................  ~40 (modules on the plates + cable strain relief)

      M3 x 10 screw + M3 nut + M3 washer .......................  ~16 sets
