## 3. What was built in this session (8–9 October)

Items and robots are reserved for overlapping PENDING/CONFIRMED bookings. Table `booking_items` (new). Code: `backend/app/services/booking_equipment.py`, `frontend/src/components/BookingEquipment.tsx`.
- **Smart bin, take-out by weight**: *Take* in the portal → OPEN_LID command → the bin weighs before/after → `POST /api/reusable/checkout-complete`; a drop ≥ 2 g records the checkout. Identical items are one line ("Breadboard · 3 of 3"); the return scan names the exact one, and a return of a different identical item closes the open checkout (staff told about the swap). Notifications to the person (Taken / Returned / Not taken) and to staff. `POST /api/reusable/weight-change`: a settled change with no request → staff alert with the likely number of items.
- **Who may take**: staff/admin always; a student inside the lab, or in their confirmed booking window after the door let them in (`may_take` in `routes/reusable.py`). The Smart-bin card also shows on the active booking's page.
- **Personal door QR** (Profile → "Door QR for your phone's Wallet"): payload `SLB:~<secret>`; only its SHA-256 is stored (`personal_qr_credentials`); same rules as an RFID card at the door, then finger/face. "SLB:" prefix so the door firmware forwards it without a reflash. Replace / revoke supported.
- **Save QR for Wallet** button (booking QR page, Profile door QR): share/download the QR image + copy its text for a pass app. A real "Add to Apple Wallet" (.pkpass) needs an Apple Developer Pass Type certificate — **parked by the owner**.
- **System Devices** page (renamed): states derived where a device does not report itself — ESP32-CAM from the door controller's `camera` component; face server probed at `FACE_SERVER_URL/users`; security camera OFFLINE when its service reports `source_ok: false`. Register types now include Robot, Lab PC, Other.
- **IoT Dashboard** (renamed from Command Center), also on port 8080/8443; signing in there lands on `/command`.
- **Hazard alarm**: no full-screen flame/smoke alarm from a node that stopped reporting (alert stays open on Alerts). Alert #244 (flame 16:46–18:13 local, 9 Oct) is open — the flame board read 1 for 1.5 h with no fire: sensitivity / IR source to check on the hardware.

**Reusable smart bin firmware** (`firmware/SmartLab_Reusable_Bin_Modular`, now `BIN_MODE FULL_SYSTEM_MODE`, `LAB_ID "LAB_01"`, device `REUSE_BIN_1`)
- PN532 on its own I2C bus **Wire1, SDA GPIO 18, SCL GPIO 19** (GPIO 16/17 stopped working after a 5 V test — do not use). Read code = exactly the door's (readPassiveTargetID, 250 ms).
- HX711 32/33 (1 kg cell, calibrated), LCD 0x27 on 21/22, servo GPIO 25 (MG90S; saved idle 90°, open 0°; MG996R arrives 10 Oct → re-save angles), MC-38 lid sensor GPIO 27 + GND (verified OPEN/CLOSED).
- Lid cycle: open → 5 s timer starts at lid-sensor OPEN → weight change ≥ 3 g for 0.3 s → wait until settled (±0.5 g for 0.5 s) → 2 s for the hand → gentle close (1°/16 ms). Power-on zero only if < 10 g after 5 s. Idle LCD: "SMART BIN / <total> g".
- Flash: `arduino-cli compile/upload --fqbn esp32:esp32:esp32 -p COM7` (arduino-cli in the Arduino IDE folder). A reset in the middle of a PN532 transfer can leave it stuck until USB is unplugged (open item).
