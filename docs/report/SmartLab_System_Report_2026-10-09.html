Intelligent IoT-Based Smart Research Laboratory

System report: what the system does, what has been reached, and every feature of the portal for students, laboratory staff and administrators
In one paragraph. The Smart Lab system runs a research laboratory end to end on its own local network. Booking the laboratory issues a QR credential; a two-step door (card or QR, then fingerprint or face) lets the person in; environment sensors, a security camera and read-only robot monitoring watch the room; smart bins track equipment and components by NFC and weight; and one web portal shows everything live to students, staff and administrators, with reports, alerts, maintenance tickets and an AI assistant.

Three rules hold everywhere:

  • Only the door controller opens the door. No network message can reach the lock; the portal only answers "whose booking is this, and is it valid here and now?".
  • The portal never invents data. Charts count recorded rows; missing data is shown as "not recorded".
  • Robots are read-only. No part of the system can send a command to a robot (enforced by an automated test).

3. The hardware subsystems

3.1 Door access control

  • Two steps: RFID card or booking QR code (read by the ESP32-CAM), then the same person's fingerprint (AS608) or face (face server on the laptop).
  • The portal validates a QR only for its laboratory and its time window; anyone else's finger or face is refused as an identity mismatch.
  • Door sensor (MC-38): forced entry, door held open and "door never opened" are detected and logged.
  • Colour screen shows the granted person's photo; a door tablet speaks prompts; the lock is locked by default at power-on.
  • Emergency modes from the portal: hold open, lockdown, evacuation alarm. The door controller remains the final authority.
  • Fail closed: if the portal cannot be reached, a booking QR is refused.

3.2 Environment monitoring

  • DHT22 temperature and humidity, two PIR motion zones, 5-channel flame sensor, MQ-2 smoke/gas indicator.
  • Flame or smoke: full-screen red alarm with siren on every open portal page. Heat or humidity over limits: advice to staff. Motion: a silent note.
  • Offline detection, with one alert per outage.

3.3 Security camera and vision

  • People detection and tracking on the laptop (YOLOX-S); each name stays on the right person through crossings.
  • Names come from the door entry and the enrolled faces: "verified" when both agree; a card-sharing alert when the face contradicts the badge.
  • Equipment boxes per PC or robot: alerts when an item is moved, missing, or moves with nobody near it.
  • Camera wall of all laboratories; events with snapshots; the stream never leaves the laptop (staff see it through the portal).
  • Latency measured at capture, analysis, rendering and serving (frame age in every response).

3.4 Robot monitoring (read-only)

  • UR5e telemetry through the receive-only RTDE interface; FANUC protocol only after the controller is inspected.
  • Per-joint movement start/stop, joint moved by how much, stale-telemetry detection (never shown as "stationary").
  • HOME position calibration with versions; fusion with the camera (motion seen in the robot's box).
  • 3D twins built from the manufacturers' models, live per-joint traces, reach ring, movement replay, tool trail, wall mode.

3.5 Reusable smart bin (equipment such as breadboards)

1 kg load cell + HX711Total weight of the bin; confirms take-out and returnGPIO 32/33

ServoOpens the lid through the pedal linkageGPIO 25, external 5 V
MC-38 lid sensorConfirms the lid really opened and closedGPIO 27 + GND

Take-out: the person presses Take in the portal → the bin opens → the weight drops by about one item (a breadboard weighs ≈ 33–35 g) → the lid closes 2 s after the weight settles → the take-out is recorded and the person is notified. Return: the person scans the item's NFC tag → the bin opens → the weight rises → recorded and notified. The tag tells exactly which item came back; identical items are taken as "a breadboard".

Safety: the lid closes on its own after 5 s without a weight change; a lid that never opens is reported; the scale zeroes itself at power-on only when the bin is empty (below 10 g), so items inside are never "forgotten".

Verified on 9 Oct 2026: scan → lid opened (sensor confirmed) → +35.4 g settled → gentle close → lid closed (sensor confirmed); portal take-out by weight recorded (−32.7 g).

3.6 Consumables scale

Weighing bins for bulk components (for example 10 kΩ resistors, red LEDs). The count is an estimate from measured mass and measured unit mass, shown only after calibration. The portal side (bins, measurements, withdrawals and refills) exists; the scale hardware is the next step.

7. End-to-end journeys

7.1 A student's lab session with a breadboard

  1. Ali books the Robotics Laboratory for 14:00–16:00, purpose "Project prototyping", equipment: a breadboard, UR5e and lab PC.
  2. The booking is confirmed and a QR credential is issued.
  3. At the door: QR code → fingerprint → door opens; the entry is logged against the booking.
  4. In the portal (booking page or laboratory page) Ali presses Take → the bin opens, LCD "Breadboard / BIN OPENING!".
  5. Ali takes one breadboard; the weight drops by ≈33 g; the lid closes; the portal records the take-out and notifies Ali; staff see "Ali took a breadboard".
  6. At the end Ali scans the breadboard's NFC tag at the bin → the lid opens → he puts it in → +33 g → recorded as "Returned: Breadboard 2"; Ali and staff are notified.

7.2 Something taken without the portal

If the bin's weight changes while no take-out or return is running, staff immediately get an alert such as "Smart bin: 32.9 g removed without a request — about 1 item".

7.3 A fire alarm

The flame sensor triggers → every open portal page shows a full-screen red alarm with a siren → staff can trigger evacuation from the Emergency page; the door logs everyone's exit.

8. Data, security and reliability

  • Everything is recorded: door events, sensor readings, alerts, bookings, camera events, bin transactions — the dataset for analysis and the thesis.
  • Data is never deleted: history is kept; database restores are insert-only merges; tests run on a separate test database; a database backup is taken before every schema change.
  • Local network only: the system works without internet (lab router 192.168.1.x, server 192.168.1.200).
  • Security: Argon2id password hashing, strong-password policy, sign-in throttling, strict browser security headers, local HTTPS certificate authority, device keys for every board, secrets never in the code repository.
  • Fail-safe hardware: the door locks at power-on and refuses QR codes when the portal is unreachable; the bin never opens without the portal's approval; servo hold time is limited.
