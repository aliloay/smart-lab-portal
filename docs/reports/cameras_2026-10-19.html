3. How it works

  1. Each camera runs its own service (person detector on the graphics card, tracker, equipment boxes, door box). A camera that fails cannot stop the other.
  2. Names come only from evidence: a door entry, or a clear face match (two agreeing votes). Nothing is guessed - an unconfirmed person stays "Unknown".
  3. Floor map: each camera turns a person's feet in the picture into a floor position in metres (same room coordinates for both cameras).
  4. Sharing: every pass each camera asks the other for its people and positions; a confirmed name on the same floor spot (within 0.7 m) is carried over.
  5. Locking: once named, the name stays on the person's track; people side by side never swap names; after a short loss the name is kept 20 s for the same spot.

4. Known limits (measured today)

  • Feet must be visible. Directly in front of camera 1 the feet leave the picture; behind desks the table hides the legs. There the position is wrong, so no name is shared (safe, but the person may be "Unknown" on one camera).
  • Doorway (permanent: camera 1 is already tilted to its limit and the FANUC cell stays): the cell partly hides people entering from camera 1, and camera 2 cannot see the doorway; item 2 (appearance matching) covers this. Camera 1 lost one track for about 1 s after the door name, then re-named it.
  • Seated people can be named on one camera and Unknown on the other.
  • Camera clocks show one hour behind (camera time zone) until 29 October; portal events use the laptop clock.
  • Eng. Hemaly cannot be named at the door yet: her face is saved but not trained on the face server.
